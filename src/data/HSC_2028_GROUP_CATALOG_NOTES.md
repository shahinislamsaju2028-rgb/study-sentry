# HSC 2028 Humanities and Business Studies catalogs

`hscGroupCatalog.json` was parsed from the two user-supplied reference files. It contains chapter names by subject and paper; chapters intentionally have empty `types` arrays where the source did not provide a structured question-type breakdown.

The supplied Humanities file cites the NCTB 2026 textbook version and advises verification against the final board/NCTB syllabus. The Business Studies file says its chapter list was compiled from non-official online textbook references and is not an official syllabus. The onboarding screen repeats these limitations. Students should verify chapter titles/order against their own books and official board notices.

The available batch-specific group data is HSC 2028. Other batches can be recorded in the profile, but are not auto-imported from this catalog.
